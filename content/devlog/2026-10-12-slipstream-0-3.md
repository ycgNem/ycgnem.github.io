A bigger run, new salvage, terrain, a boss, and two more bodies to drive. [Play it here](/games/slipstream-salvager/).

## The Compactor

The last yard is now a boss. It wears a Booster, Springs and a Plow, and each part is one of its moves. Tear one off and that move is gone, and the part is yours. Strip it bare and its core opens.

## Two new enemies, two new parts

- **The Hopper** paints a red ring where it will land, leaps over everything and slams down. Then it's stuck: hit its back to take its **Jump Jets**.
- **The Bouncer** rolls at you, bouncing off walls and getting faster. Cut across it to take its **Springs**, and you bounce off walls instead of clanking.

## Terrain

Boost strips, oil (no grip), jump ramps, and crusher plates you can lure enemies onto. Five new yards use them: The Hurdles, The Pinball, The Slick, The Jumps and The Press.

## The Garage

PLAY now opens the Garage. Two new chassis unlock by playing, each by its own move: **the Skiff** (catch 15 slipstreams) never slows in a turn, and **the Hauler** (cancel 15 windups) stops a windup just by bumping into it.

There's also a **daily run**: one seed a day, the same for everyone.

Full notes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/ycgNem/slipstream-salvager/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
