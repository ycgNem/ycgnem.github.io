Two things today: a brand-new game, and a big update to Overgrowth.

## Pixel RTS

A top-down pixel-art real-time strategy game, inspired by The Conquerors 3 and Strategema on Roblox. [Play it here](/games/pixel-rts/).

Start with an HQ, mine ore and pump oil, build barracks, factories, airfields and docks, capture and grow cities, and research a tech tree of 29 techs across four eras. Win by conquest, by science, or by holding the map.

- **Skirmish** against up to five AIs, each with its own personality, at three difficulties.
- **Online** with up to six players. The host's browser runs the game and friends join with a room code. There's no game server and nothing to install.
- **Boot Camp** walks new players through the basics, and the Field Guide covers every unit and building.

It has twists of its own too: a government in exile, a last stand at the HQ, insurgency for knocked-out players, city contract auctions and bounties, and wanted aces.

It's an early prototype. Tell me what breaks.

## Overgrowth 0.5: Sharp Edges

Overgrowth is now a gather, craft and defend game. By day you strip the jungle for materials; by night you hold the Heartwood against the Blight until dawn. Survive to night 10 to win. [Play it here](/games/overgrowth/).

![Overgrowth 0.5: the Heartwood at camp, machete in hand](/assets/games/overgrowth/cover.webp)

0.5 is about the machete:

- A real machete in a gloved hand, with forehand and backhand swings.
- Every blow cuts a notch into the bark, and hacking the same spot opens it wider.
- The blade sinks into wood and has to be wrenched free; on rock it bounces back.
- The forest floor is now a mix of leaves, bare earth, roots and seedlings instead of one carpet.

Full notes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/ycgNem/overgrowth/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
