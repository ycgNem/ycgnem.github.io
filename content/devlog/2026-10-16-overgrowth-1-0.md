Overgrowth is at 1.0. It folds four planned updates into one release.

[Play it here](/games/overgrowth/).

## The day is a raid

- **Weight.** Everything you carry weighs something. Past 70% of your pack you're Laden, past 100% you're Encumbered: no sprinting, a slow walk and loud feet.
- **Containers you search.** Crates, lockers, urns, reliquaries, sunken chests and root caches hidden in buttress roots. Their slots reveal one by one, slower for rarer things. Locked chests need a pry bar, or a long, loud forced opening.
- **The Coffer** at camp holds 60 slots with no weight limit, and crafting at camp draws from it.
- **Benches.** Towers, walls and packs come from the Workbench, braziers and mortars from the Fire Pit, totems and scrap work from the Tinker's Bench.
- **Dying by day drops your pack** as a lost satchel with a beacon. Get back to it before the horn.
- **Husks** roam from day 3 and hunt by sound: sprinting, a heavy pack and breaking a crate all carry.

![Overgrowth: searching a supply crate from the satchel](/assets/games/overgrowth/shot5.webp)

## A defence you have to think about

- **Root capacity.** The Heartwood can feed only so many roots. A barricade draws 1, a bow tower 3, a mortar 4, and each upgrade tier one more.
- **Damage types.** Pierce, blunt, fire and arc. Rotboars shrug off arrows but break under mortar shells, and Husks go up like kindling.
- **Elite Blight** from night 5 on Warden, and from night 3 the packs lean on the rift your towers cover least. The game tells you which side they're testing.

## A story in ten nights

- **Twelve torn pages** from a 1931 expedition are scattered through the forest. Two of them are maps.
- **Three seals** in the temple, the shrine and the ruins. Set a relic in one and its rift sleeps for two nights.
- **Grafts** for your towers, **Hollis's radio** that calls supply drops at noon, and **the Rot**, dying ground that spreads from the rifts after night 5.
- **The Rot Mother** owns night 10, and the rescue plane comes at first light: fly out, or stay with the Heartwood.

## The rest

Rebind any key, captions for the sounds that matter, colour-blind outlines for the Blight, partial gamepad support, twenty achievements, a daily vigil that's the same world for everyone, and a capture mode on F8. The models download at half the size.

## 1.0.1: Lighter Loads

Everything weighs about half as much (a log is 1.5 kg, was 4) and every pack carries more, so a full day's foraging fits before you're encumbered. Smashing a crate with the machete now spills its loot on the ground instead of deleting it.
