Tonight's builds, all three in playtest.

## Overgrowth 0.8 preview: The Long Vigil, part one

The first half of the next update, live early. [Play it here](/games/overgrowth/).

- **Spitters** (from night 3) stop behind the pack and lob burning bile at your towers. Run them down with the machete.
- **Burrowers** (from night 6) tunnel under your walls and burst up on the far side. Nothing can hit them underground.
- **The Glowcap Lantern** answers both: Blight in its light take 25% more damage, and a Burrower tunnelling into it gets dragged up and dazed.
- **Memories** are permanent unlocks earned from your lifetime records, like starting with a slingshot after you hold through night 3, or a fourth boon card after you fell 150 Blight.
- **Nights are actually dark now.** Fires, lanterns and the Heartwood light the clearing.

Full notes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/ycgNem/overgrowth/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## Pixel RTS: First Orders

Outside Boot Camp, the opening wasn't explaining itself. Now every newcomer's match starts with **First Orders**: a guide that names the very next click and points at it with an arrow on the map or a blinking button. It covers your first mine, research, a city, oil, a Barracks and a Factory, then gets out of the way. [Play it here](/games/pixel-rts/).

## Verdant Relay: legibility and foraging

Stage 8 is a readability pass: bigger text, crisper digits and icons, and a cleaner night sidebar. It also adds foraging: wild fruit that heals, glints of scrap in the brush, and dusk blooms worth Intel and research. Forage you've seen stays marked on the map. [Play it here](/games/verdant-relay/).
