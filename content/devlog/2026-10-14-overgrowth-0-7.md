**Pixel RTS and Verdant Relay are now in playtest**, alongside Arcwarm and Overgrowth. That means they're ready for playtesters: play them, and tell me what worked and what didn't.

## Overgrowth 0.6 and 0.7

[Play it here](/games/overgrowth/).

**0.6: Blight Blooms.** Every dawn, a few glowing pods sprout out in the forest, each guarded by Blightlings. Cut one down for rare loot and favour with the Heartwood. Leave it, and at dusk it opens a new lane onto the Heart all night. So each day is a choice: hunt the blooms, or build to cover the flank they'll open. 0.6 also added grass meadows, forest clutter to pick up, and a better sprint.

**0.7: The Title.** Overgrowth now opens like a finished game:

- A live title screen, with the camera drifting round the Heartwood at sunset and its own title music
- Three difficulties: Verdant, Warden and Blightborn
- A Field Guide with portraits, stats and lore for every Blight and defence
- Lifetime records across every run
- A proper pause menu, and a menu that works from the keyboard

Full notes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/ycgNem/overgrowth/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
