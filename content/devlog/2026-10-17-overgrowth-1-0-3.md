Two patches landed on top of [Overgrowth 1.0](/devlog/overgrowth-1-0/).

[Play it here](/games/overgrowth/).

## 1.0.2: Smooth Going

- **No more random freezes.** The game used to stop for up to half a second the first time you held a defence, the first time each kind of loot dropped, and the first time you held a poultice or the slingshot. All of that is now prepared on the loading screen.
- **Crafting out in the forest works again.** Field recipes could fail with an error away from camp.
- **The belt takes anything** when the pack is full, and the game tells you whether you're out of pockets or a thing is too heavy.
- **Smashed-crate loot comes to you**, so nothing lands out of reach.

## 1.0.3: Iron and Order

- **Drag and drop in the satchel.** Drag a stack onto any slot, or off the satchel to drop it. Click a stack to send it into the open Coffer or container and back. Ctrl takes half a stack, and 1–8 puts the hovered stack on the belt.
- **Sort buttons** for your pack and the Coffer.
- **Dropping works properly.** Right-click drops one, Shift+right-click drops the stack, and what you drop stays on the ground until you pick it up.
- **Salvaging a defence is a held right-click**, with a ring that fills, so one stray click no longer breaks a tower down.
- **Upgrade the Coffer.** Clad it in iron for 90 slots (4 scrap metal, 4 rusty bolts, 2 bog iron), then bind it in brass for 120.
- **The camp faces you.** The Coffer and the benches face out toward the forest, where you come in from.
- **The Blight are 20% tougher.** Every kind has a fifth more health and hits a fifth harder, on every difficulty, bosses included.
- **Fewer stutters at dawn.** On a CPU slowed to a quarter speed, the worst dawn frame went from about 200 ms to about 65 ms.

Full notes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/ycgNem/overgrowth/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
